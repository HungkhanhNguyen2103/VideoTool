# Bộ Style Reference cho 6 phong cách hình ảnh của Video Tool (bản trung tính)

Mục đích: mỗi phong cách có bộ 5 ảnh style reference để thêm vào "Project Style References" của Runner (Flow). Cùng một phong cách vẽ nhưng khác bố cục, cỡ cảnh và ánh sáng.

**Nguyên tắc trung tính:** vì mọi ảnh trong bộ này đều gửi cho mọi scene, các chủ đề cố ý KHÔNG chứa giáp, quân phục, lều, cờ, ấn hay kiến trúc của một nền văn hóa hay thời đại nào. Trang phục và bối cảnh đặc thù (bộ đội 1954, lính Pháp, lính Mỹ, nhà tranh, hầm hào) do prompt scene và Scene Reference quyết định.

## Cách dùng
1. Mở Flow, tạo ảnh bằng Nano Banana, khung 16:9.
2. Mỗi ảnh = STYLE BLOCK + một dòng SUBJECT (nút "Sao chép prompt" trong form tạo dự án làm sẵn việc ghép này).
3. Tạo cả 5 ảnh của một phong cách trong cùng một phiên Flow; mỗi ảnh tạo 3–4 lần rồi chọn bản đẹp nhất.
4. Thêm vào Runner theo thứ tự: core (đặt Primary), wide, interior, night, props.
5. Thử với vài scene không liên quan đến bộ ảnh để kiểm tra độ đa dạng.
6. Chưa có nhãn trong Runner thì chỉ dùng 4 ảnh (bỏ props).

## Cinematic (điện ảnh)

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A still frame from a high-end cinematic historical documentary: naturalistic but dramatic lighting (window light, firelight, overcast sky, low sun), shallow depth of field, muted desaturated color grading, photographic texture in fabric, metal, dust and skin. Favor wide and medium compositions. No readable text, no logos, no real-person likeness.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a person in plain everyday clothing standing on a hilltop at overcast midday, looking across a valley, soft natural light. |
| wide | Wide establishing shot of a river valley at low sun with a few small distant buildings, long shadows, atmospheric haze. |
| interior | Interior of a plain candle-lit room, two people in everyday clothing leaning over a wooden table with a rolled paper, window light on faces, shallow depth of field. |
| night | A small group of people crossing a shallow river at dusk, firelight on the bank reflected in the water, low angle, dust and smoke. |
| props | Close-up of a rolled paper, an oil lantern, a wooden box and a coil of rope on a worn wooden table, side light, very shallow depth of field. |

## Anime

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A clean modern anime illustration: confident linework, expressive but readable faces, cel-shaded color blocks and a cohesive palette. Clear silhouettes and a purposeful focal point, with backgrounds and props detailed enough to carry the period and setting. No readable text, no logos.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a person in plain everyday clothing on a hilltop, wind in their clothes, looking across a valley, soft daylight, expressive determined face. |
| wide | Wide establishing shot of a river valley with a few small buildings and a few small figures on a road, detailed painted-style background, clear sky. |
| interior | Interior of a plain room with two people at a wooden table with a rolled paper, warm lantern glow, one person's face in close emphasis. |
| night | Dynamic low-angle shot of a group of people crossing a river at dusk by firelight, strong diagonal composition, motion lines in the water, dramatic orange and blue contrast. |
| props | Still-life close-up of a rolled paper, an oil lantern and a wooden box on a table, clean linework, soft rim light. |

---

## Watercolor (màu nước)

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A soft watercolor painting on textured paper: visible brush strokes, transparent layered washes, gentle pigment variation and natural bleeding at the edges, unpainted paper showing where the light falls. Atmospheric composition that preserves key forms and mood. No readable text, no logos.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a person in plain clothing on a hilltop looking across a valley, loose washes, calm overcast mood. |
| wide | Very wide valley landscape with a few small distant buildings dissolving into mist, large soft washes, a few tiny figures. |
| interior | Interior of a plain room with two figures bending over a table, warm lantern wash against cool shadows, loose edges. |
| night | A group of people crossing a river at dusk, firelight glowing as warm blooms of pigment into deep blue washes, expressive brush strokes. |
| props | Loose still life of a rolled paper, an oil lantern and a wooden box on a table, plenty of bare paper, delicate pigment bleeding. |

## Photorealistic (ảnh thực)

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A documentary-style photograph: true-to-life color, physically plausible available light, accurate materials and natural skin and surface texture, captured on a real camera with a believable lens, depth and perspective. No illustration, no stylization, no heavy color grading, no glamour lighting. No readable text, no logos, no real-person likeness.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a person in plain everyday clothing standing on a hillside in flat overcast light, natural skin texture, 50mm lens look. |
| wide | Wide landscape photograph of a river valley with a few small distant buildings, natural afternoon light, 24mm lens look. |
| interior | Interior of a plain room lit by one lantern and daylight through the window, two people at a wooden table with a rolled paper, 35mm lens look. |
| night | Group of people wading a shallow river at dusk by firelight, low angle, motion in the water, high-ISO photographic grain. |
| props | Macro-style photograph of a rolled paper, an oil lantern and a wooden box on a wooden table, natural window light. |

---

## Flat Cartoon

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A flat, cel-shaded cartoon illustration with bold clean black outlines, flat color fills and only minimal, simple shadow blocks; no photorealistic lighting, textures or gradients. Characters have slightly chibi-like proportions with somewhat oversized heads, simplified expressive faces and compact bodies. Muted, desaturated earthy palette of olive greens, tans, browns and dusty blues. No readable text, no logos.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a single person in plain everyday clothing standing on a hilltop, looking across a valley, calm overcast daylight, figure left of center. |
| wide | Very wide establishing shot of a river valley with a few small buildings far away and a few tiny figures on a road, deep perspective, empty foreground. |
| interior | Interior of a plain room: a large wooden table with a rolled paper and a lantern, two people in discussion, warm lantern light, medium-close composition. |
| night | Low-angle dramatic shot of a group of people crossing a shallow river at dusk, firelight on the bank, motion in the water, strong diagonal composition. |
| props | Still-life close-up on a wooden table: a rolled paper, an oil lantern, a wooden box and a coil of rope, shallow depth, no readable text. |

---

## Strategic Map (2D)

**STYLE BLOCK:**
A 2D vector illustration with flat colors, bold clean outlines, flat lighting and minimal shadow; no photorealistic lighting, complex shading or gradients. Earth-tone terrain (greens, browns, tans, muted blues for water) and distinct solid colors for each faction. Simplified characters with highly exaggerated, readable facial expressions. No readable text or numbers, no logos.

| Nhãn | SUBJECT |
|---|---|
| core | Medium shot of a person at a table with a confident, exaggerated expression, simplified character, plain background. |
| map | Top-down tactical map with two color-coded territories, a river and hills, and directional arrows showing one force advancing; no labels. |
| map-alt | Top-down tactical map where two factions' arrows converge on a river crossing, different terrain layout from the previous map; no labels. |
| combat | Wide shot of simplified figures and effects clashing on a plain, bold shapes, clear sides by color, exaggerated shocked expressions. |
| infographic | Tangible metaphor of resources: tall stacks of sacks on one side and a small pile on the other, flat colors, no numbers or text. |
