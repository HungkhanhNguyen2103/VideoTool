## Style
Render every scene as a 2D vector illustration with flat colors, bold clean outlines, flat lighting, and minimal shadow; avoid photorealistic lighting, complex shading, and gradients. Alternate two scene families across the video: MACRO, a top-down tactical map with clearly color-coded territories and forces plus directional arrows; and MICRO, a medium shot of simplified characters with highly exaggerated, readable facial expressions such as confident, anxious, or shocked. Use earth tones for terrain—greens, browns, tans, and muted blues for water—and distinct solid colors for each faction on maps, keeping faction colors consistent throughout the video.

Match the narration to the shot: show territory or strategy as a map with arrows, a leadership decision as a medium shot of the commander at a table or vantage point, combat as a wide shot of simplified figures and effects, and statistics as an infographic or tangible metaphor such as stacks or piles rather than dense text. Use documents and orders as stylized props like scrolls or sealed letters; readable text and numbers do not belong inside generated images unless requested. Avoid real-person likenesses, and simplify historical costumes, vessels, and weapons while keeping their shapes period-plausible.
