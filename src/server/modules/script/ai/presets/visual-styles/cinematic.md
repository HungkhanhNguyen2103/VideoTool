## Style
Render every scene as a still frame from a high-end cinematic historical documentary: naturalistic but dramatic lighting (window light, firelight, overcast sky, low sun), shallow depth of field, muted desaturated color grading, and photographic texture in fabric, metal, dust and skin. Favor wide and medium compositions, and use close details of hands, objects or faces only for emotional emphasis.
