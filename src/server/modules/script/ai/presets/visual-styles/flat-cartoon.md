## Style
Render every scene as a flat, cel-shaded cartoon illustration with bold clean black outlines, flat color fills and only minimal, simple shadow blocks; no photorealistic lighting, textures or gradients. Give characters slightly chibi-like proportions with somewhat oversized heads, simplified expressive faces and compact bodies. Use a muted, desaturated earthy palette of olive greens, tans, browns and dusty blues, and keep it consistent from scene to scene.
