## Style
Render every scene as a flat, cel-shaded cartoon illustration with bold clean black outlines and no photorealistic lighting or gradients. Use flat color fills with only minimal, simple shadow blocking; give characters slightly chibi-like proportions with somewhat oversized heads, simplified expressive faces, and compact bodies. Favor a muted, desaturated earthy palette of olive greens, tans, browns, and dusty blues.
